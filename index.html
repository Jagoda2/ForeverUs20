03 · US

Then there
was us.

14 · 06 · 2025

A few weeks after the cinema, I asked you out. You declined. We were coursemates, and it became a lot for me to handle, so I tried to draw back.

Then you reached out a few weeks later — that maybe we should give it a shot.

And on June 14, 2025, we stopped wondering what we could be and became us.

July 21 brought your birthday gift to me. Then September 2 brought a difficult chapter, and we stopped talking.
